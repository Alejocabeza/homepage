### Holistic Product Vision

As a Full-Stack Developer, my main strength is the ability to oversee and execute a software project from conception to deployment and maintenance. I understand how the frontend, backend, database, and infrastructure interact, which allows me to make technical decisions that benefit the product as a whole.

This comprehensive vision is especially valuable for startups and small teams, where agility and the ability to pivot quickly are key.

### Frontend

Although my specialty is the backend, I have strong competence in modern frontend development, which allows me to build interactive and efficient user interfaces.

- **Frameworks and Libraries:** I have experience with **React** and **Next.js** to create dynamic web applications with server-side rendering (SSR) for optimal performance and SEO. I have also worked with **Astro** for content-oriented and high-speed sites.
- **Styling:** I am proficient in **Tailwind CSS** for rapid and consistent utility-based development. I can also work with plain CSS, Sass, and other preprocessors.
- **Application State:** I have implemented state management solutions like Redux for complex applications that require a predictable data flow.

### Backend

The backend is where the main business logic resides and where I apply my deepest expertise. (For more details, see my services for "Backend Software Architecture" and "API Development").

- **Languages and Frameworks:** **PHP** (Laravel, Symfony) and **Node.js** (NestJS, Express).
- **Databases:** **PostgreSQL** and **MySQL**.

### Frontend-Backend Connection

The true magic of Full-Stack happens in the integration between the client and the server.

- **APIs:** I design and consume efficient RESTful and GraphQL APIs for smooth communication.
- **Authentication:** I implement complete authentication flows, from the login form on the frontend to token validation (JWT) on the backend.
- **Optimization:** I apply techniques like lazy loading of components and data pagination to ensure the application feels fast and responsive, regardless of the amount of data it handles.
